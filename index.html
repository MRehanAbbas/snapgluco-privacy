🩺 SnapGluco Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

⚠️ Medical Disclaimer: SnapGluco is a health information tool only. It is NOT a medical device and does NOT replace professional medical advice. Always consult your doctor for diabetes management decisions.

1. About SnapGluco

SnapGluco ("we", "us", "our") is an AI-powered diabetic food scanner app that helps users track their food intake, glycemic index scores, and blood sugar impact. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Email address and name when you create an account
  • Health data: Diabetes type, age, weight, height, daily sugar goals
  • Meal logs: Food names, calories, GI scores, nutritional information
  • Blood sugar readings: Readings you manually enter
  • Food photos: Temporarily sent to Google Gemini AI for analysis only — never stored
  • Device information: Device type and OS version for app optimization

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide personalized diabetes management features
  • Calculate GI scores and blood sugar predictions
  • Track your nutrition and health progress
  • Sync your data across your devices
  • Improve app features and accuracy
  • Send optional health reminders

We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in two ways:

  • Local storage: All meal logs and preferences stored securely on your device
  • Cloud storage: When signed in, data is stored in Supabase (GDPR-compliant, encrypted database)

All data transmission uses SSL/TLS encryption. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

5. AI Food Analysis

When you scan food:

  • Your photo is temporarily sent to Google Gemini AI for real-time analysis
  • The image is processed and immediately discarded — not stored
  • Results are saved locally on your device only
  • See Google's AI privacy policy: ai.google.dev/privacy

6. Third-Party Services

We use these third-party services:

  • Google Gemini AI: Food image analysis — Google Privacy Policy
  • Supabase: Cloud data storage and authentication — Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Advertising for free users — Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management — RevenueCat Privacy Policy

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of analytics

8. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data retained while account is active
  • Deleted accounts: Data permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Anonymous users: Data on device only, deleted when app is uninstalled

9. Children's Privacy

SnapGluco is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

10. GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Union, we comply with GDPR requirements. You have the right to data portability, erasure, and restriction of processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via in-app notification or email. Continued use of SnapGluco after changes indicates acceptance.
